Our story

Lugard Dishes is a kitchen built around the food many of us grew up on: slow-cooked soups, charcoal grills, smoky jollof and fish straight from the water. Everything is cooked to order, with pepper on the side when you want it.

Come hungry, share a few plates, and try something you have not had before. That is what the dish finder is for.
